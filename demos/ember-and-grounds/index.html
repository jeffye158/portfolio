Our Story

Crafted with Passion

Ember & Grounds was born from a simple belief: that a great cup of coffee can transform an ordinary morning into something worth remembering. We source our beans directly from farmers who share our obsession with quality.

Every roast is a conversation between the bean and the flame — a process we've refined over years of quiet dedication.

🌱

Direct Trade

Sourced directly from farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Fair prices, traceable origins.

🔥

Slow Roasted

Small-batch roasting in-house, every morning. No stale beans — ever.

🎨

Latte Art

Our baristas train for months to perfect the pour — because beauty matters in every detail.
